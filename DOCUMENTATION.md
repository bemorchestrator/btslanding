## 🎯 Marketing & Content Strategy (Boss Requirements)

**Primary Message:** DepEd-compliant lesson planning and documentation tools that save Filipino teachers days of work every week—starting with a ₱99 7-day trial.

2. **Time Savings (Quantified)**
   - Emphasize concrete time saved: "What used to take 10+ hours now takes minutes"
   - Focus on weekend recovery: "Spend your weekends resting, not on paperwork"

## 📚 What is Better Teaching Solutions?

Better Teaching Solutions is a comprehensive educational management platform designed specifically for Philippine schools following the Department of Education (DepEd) curriculum. It helps teachers, administrators, and school staff manage their daily tasks efficiently while ensuring full compliance with DepEd requirements.

Think of it as your digital assistant that handles all the paperwork, student records, lesson planning, and administrative tasks that typically take hours each day—now available at your fingertips.

### Why Use Better Teaching Solutions?

- **Save Time**: Automate repetitive tasks like form filling, attendance tracking, and grade computation
- **Stay Compliant**: All official DepEd forms (SF1-SF10) are built-in and always up-to-date
- **Work Anywhere**: Access your classroom from any device with an internet connection
- **Reduce Errors**: Automated calculations and validation ensure accuracy
- **Go Paperless**: Store and manage everything digitally with secure cloud backup
- **AI-Powered**: Get intelligent assistance for lesson planning and curriculum alignment

## 📖 How to Use - Common Tasks

### Taking Daily Attendance

1. From your Dashboard, click on your classroom
2. Go to "Attendance Tracker"
3. Select today's date (pre-selected by default)
4. Mark each student as Present (✓), Absent (✗), Late (L), or Excused (E)
5. Or click "Mark All Present" to mark everyone at once
6. Changes save automatically
7. Review attendance summary at the bottom

**Tips:**
- Use keyboard shortcuts for faster input
- Click on a student name to see their attendance history
- Export attendance reports anytime for records

---

### Generating School Form 1 (SF1)

1. Navigate to Forms → SF1 (School Register)
2. Select your classroom from the dropdown
3. Choose the school year
4. Review the auto-filled student list
5. Verify all information is correct
6. Click "Print" or "Export to PDF"
7. Download or send to printer

**What Gets Filled Automatically:**
- All enrolled students
- Student personal information
- Parent/guardian names
- Address details
- Ages and birthdates

---

### Recording Grades

1. Go to Forms → Class Record
2. Select subject, classroom, and quarter
3. Click on a cell to enter a score
4. Enter scores for:
   - Written Works (tests, quizzes)
   - Performance Tasks (projects)
   - Quarterly Assessment (final exam)
5. Grades compute automatically
6. Click "Save" to store changes
7. Export or print when needed

**Grade Computation:**
- The system follows DepEd's K-12 grading system
- Weighted percentages apply automatically
- Final grade rounds to nearest whole number
- Passing grade is 75

---

### Creating a Lesson Plan

1. Go to Tools → Lesson Plan Generator
2. Click "Create New Lesson Plan"
3. Fill in the details:
   - Subject
   - Grade level
   - Quarter and week
   - Topic/competency
4. Click "Generate with AI"
5. Wait for AI to create your lesson plan (30-60 seconds)
6. Review the generated plan
7. Make any edits or adjustments
8. Click "Save" to store it
9. Print or export as needed

**Customization Options:**
- Edit any section of the plan
- Add your own activities
- Modify suggested materials
- Change assessment methods
- Save as template for reuse

---

### Managing Multiple Classrooms

If you teach multiple classes:

1. Use the **Classroom Selector** (graduation cap icon) at the bottom-right
2. Click to open the classroom list
3. Select the classroom you want to work with
4. The current classroom shows a "Current" badge
5. All data will now relate to the selected classroom
6. Switch classrooms anytime using the same button

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: We recommend Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or Safari (latest versions). Internet Explorer is not supported.

## 📝 Glossary of Terms

**LRN (Learner Reference Number)**: A unique 12-digit number assigned to every Filipino student

**SF Forms**: School Forms required by DepEd for official records and reporting

**MATATAG Curriculum**: The updated K-12 curriculum framework introduced by DepEd

**WW (Written Works)**: Component of grading based on tests, quizzes, and written outputs (30%)

**PT (Performance Tasks)**: Component based on projects and demonstrations (50%)

**QA (Quarterly Assessment)**: End-of-quarter examination component (20%)

**DLL (Daily Lesson Log)**: A record of daily teaching activities and lessons

**Classroom**: A group of students assigned to a teacher for a specific grade and section

**School Year**: The academic year period (e.g., 2024-2025)

**Quarter**: One of four grading periods in a school year
